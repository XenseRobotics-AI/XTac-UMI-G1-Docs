    XTac UMI G1¶

    Xense XTac UMI G1 is a wearable data-collection gripper system for building embodied manipulation datasets. The leader gripper integrates tri-color visuotactile sensors, a wrist fisheye camera and a high-precision pose sensor, capturing vision, touch, pose, gripper state and IMU data in sync; the follower gripper mounts on the robot's end effector to execute or replay motions. The system serves imitation learning, VLA / VTLA model training and tactile world model research.

    System components¶

    Part Role
    Leader grippers ×2 (left / right) Handheld demonstration; each carries two visuotactile sensors, a wrist fisheye camera, an opening encoder and an IMU, see Gripper connection and serial numbers
    Pico4 Ultra Enterprise trackers ×2 Mounted on top of the leader grippers, giving their 6-DoF pose
    Pico4 Ultra Enterprise headset Tracks the trackers and sets the world frame; can also record a first-person stereo view, see Pico4 headset and tracker setup
    Collection unit The backpack on the Backpack Kit, your own collection PC on the Developer Kit, see Product line and configuration comparison
    Follower grippers (optional) Mounted on a robot to execute or replay motions, see Follower gripper overview

    Specifications are in Technical specifications.

    Highlights¶

    • Visuotactile fusion, real interaction

      High-frame-rate tri-color visuotactile sensors record contact location, grasp stability and slip trends in sync.

    • Close to the human hand, faster collection

      The wearable leader gripper is light and easy to pick up, covering grasping, carrying, insertion, thin-part picking and more.

    • High-precision pose, high-quality data

      Multi-device time sync of 5ms and positioning accuracy of < 3mm; every stream is aligned to the same instant.

    • Mainstream ecosystem, data ready to use

      Native support for LeRobot, MCAP and other mainstream formats and tools, so data is ready for training as soon as it is collected.

    • Light deployment, built for scale

      A light leader gripper and a wearable collection unit, ready for the lab, home or field.

    • Compliant fingertips for fine grasping

      Slim, compliant fingertips make small parts, thin sheets and edge-lying objects easier to pick up.

    Applications¶

    • Embodied-AI data factories: large-scale manipulation data collection across many stations, operators and tasks.
    • Robot algorithm R&D: a source of real interaction data for robotics companies, university labs and embodied-AI teams.
    • Home and service robots: tidying desks, picking and placing items, opening containers, using tools.
    • Flexible industrial manipulation: 3C assembly, small-part sorting, cable insertion, part picking.
    • Tactile and real-interaction research: contact-state understanding, tactile representation and tactile foundation models.
