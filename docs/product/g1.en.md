# XTac UMI G1

Xense XTac UMI G1 is a wearable data-collection gripper system for building embodied manipulation datasets. The leader gripper integrates tri-color visuotactile sensors, a wrist fisheye camera and a high-precision pose sensor, capturing vision, touch, pose, gripper state and IMU data in sync; the follower gripper mounts on the robot's end effector to execute or replay motions. The system serves imitation learning, VLA / VTLA model training and tactile world model research.

![XTac UMI G1 product photo](../assets/product/xtac-umi-g1-hero.webp){ width="720" }

## System components

| Part | Role |
|---|---|
| Leader grippers ×2 (left / right) | Handheld demonstration; each carries two visuotactile sensors, a wrist fisheye camera, an opening encoder and an IMU, see [Gripper connection and serial numbers](../common/gripper.md) |
| Pico4 Ultra Enterprise trackers ×2 | Mounted on top of the leader grippers, giving their 6-DoF pose |
| Pico4 Ultra Enterprise headset | Tracks the trackers and sets the world frame; can also record a first-person stereo view, see [Pico4 headset and tracker setup](../common/pico4.md) |
| Collection unit | The [backpack](../backpack/index.md) on the Backpack Kit, your own collection PC on the Developer Kit, see [Product line and configuration comparison](editions.md) |
| Follower grippers (optional) | Mounted on a robot to execute or replay motions, see [Follower gripper overview](../follower/index.md) |

Specifications are in [Technical specifications](specs.md).

- **Embodied-AI data factories**: large-scale manipulation data collection across many stations, operators and tasks.
- **Robot algorithm R&D**: a source of real interaction data for robotics companies, university labs and embodied-AI teams.
- **Home and service robots**: tidying desks, picking and placing items, opening containers, using tools.
- **Flexible industrial manipulation**: 3C assembly, small-part sorting, cable insertion, part picking.
- **Tactile and real-interaction research**: contact-state understanding, tactile representation and tactile foundation models.
